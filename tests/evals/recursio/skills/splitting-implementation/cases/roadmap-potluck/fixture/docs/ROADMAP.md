# Roadmap

- **Dietary tags**: guests mark a dish vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or nut-free, and the sheet
  shows how many dishes each guest with a restriction can eat.
- **Reminder emails**: two days before the meal, every guest who signed up gets a reminder of what
  they said they would bring; guests can opt out.
- **Waitlist**: a host can cap the number of guests; sign-ups past the cap join a waitlist and move
  up, in order, when someone cancels.
- **Course balance**: when a course has more than twice as many dishes as the emptiest one, the
  sign-up form suggests the emptiest course first.
