# MT940 (SWIFT customer statement)

Many European banks export MT940 as `.sta` or `.940`. Text in fields: a field starts with a tag
`:NN:` or `:NNa:` at the start of a line and runs on over following lines until the next tag line or
a line that is exactly `-`.

`sniff`: True when one of the first ten lines starts with `:20:`.

## Statements

A file holds one or more statements. Each starts at a `:20:` line and ends at a line that is exactly
`-`, or at the end of the file. SWIFT envelope text before `:20:` (`{1:...}{2:...}{4:`) and a closing
`-}` are ignored.

- `:20:` (reference), `:25:` (account), `:28C:` (statement number), `:64:` and `:65:` (available
  balances) are ignored.
- `:60F:` or `:60M:` -- opening balance: a mark `C` or `D` (credit or debit), a date `YYMMDD`, a
  three-letter currency, and an amount with a decimal comma: `:60F:C260301EUR1234,56`. The currency
  applies to every transaction in the statement.
- `:61:` -- one statement line per transaction (layout below).
- `:86:` -- details for the `:61:` just before it (below).
- `:62F:` or `:62M:` -- closing balance, laid out like the opening balance.

## `:61:` layout

```text
:61:VALUEDATE[ENTRYDATE]MARK[FUNDSCODE]AMOUNT TYPE REFERENCE[//BANKREF]
```

written with no spaces:

- VALUEDATE: six digits, `YYMMDD` (YY means 20YY); it becomes `day`.
- ENTRYDATE: four digits, `MMDD`, optional; ignored.
- MARK: `C` credit, `D` debit, `RC` reversal of a credit (money out), `RD` reversal of a debit
  (money in). D and RC give a negative amount, C and RD a positive one.
- FUNDSCODE: one optional letter (the third letter of the currency code), present only when the
  character after the mark is a letter.
- AMOUNT: digits with a decimal comma, at most 15 characters: `12,00`, `1234,5` or `1234,`.
- TYPE: `N` or `F` and three letters or digits (`NTRF`, `NMSC`, `FCHG`); ignored.
- REFERENCE: up to 16 characters, up to `//` or the end of the line; it becomes `ref`, except that
  `NONREF` gives no ref (`None`). BANKREF and any line after the first are ignored.

## `:86:` details

Either plain text or, as German banks write it, structured with `?NN` subfields after a three-digit
business code (`:86:166?00GUTSCHRIFT?20...`). It is structured when it contains `?`.

- Structured: the field's lines are joined with no separator. Payee = `?32` followed by `?33`; when
  there is no `?32`, payee = `?00`. Memo = `?20` to `?29`, joined in order. `?30`, `?31` and other
  subfields are ignored.
- Plain: the field's lines are joined with one space. Payee = the text before the first `/` (or the
  whole text when there is none), trimmed; memo = the text after that `/`, trimmed (or `""`).
- A `:61:` with no `:86:` after it has payee `""` and memo `""`.

## Balance check

A statement's opening balance plus all its transaction amounts must equal its closing balance (a
`D` balance is negative). When it does not, the whole statement is skipped: one `Skipped` on its
`:20:` line with the reason `balance mismatch`, and none of its transactions are returned. A
statement with no opening or no closing balance is skipped the same way, with the reason
`missing balance`. Later statements are still read.

## Malformed

A `:61:` that does not match the layout is skipped on its own line, with its `:86:`. A statement with
a skipped `:61:` is not balance-checked; its other transactions are returned.

## Example

```text
:20:STMT0301
:25:DE44500105175407324931
:28C:12/1
:60F:C260301EUR100,00
:61:2603010301D3,50NTRFNONREF//BK1
:86:166?00KARTENZAHLUNG?20Coffee?21 morning?32Cafe Uno
:61:260302C1200,NTRFSAL-03
:86:Salary March/ACME GmbH
:62F:C260302EUR1296,50
-
```

## Writing

`dump(transactions, account, opening=Decimal("0"))` writes one statement for all the transactions,
which must share one currency (else `FormatError`), in order:

- `:20:LEDGERKIT`, `:25:` the account, `:28C:1/1`;
- `:60F:` the opening balance, dated the first transaction's day (`C` for 0 or more, else `D`,
  then the amount's absolute value with a decimal comma and always two decimals);
- per transaction a `:61:` line -- the value date, `C` or `D`, the absolute amount with a decimal
  comma and two decimals, `NTRF`, then the ref or `NONREF` -- and an `:86:` with the payee, a `/`,
  and the memo (the payee alone when the memo is empty), plain, never structured. `:86:` text
  longer than 65 characters runs on over further lines of at most 65 characters each;
- `:62F:` the closing balance, opening plus the amounts, dated the last transaction's day; then
  a line `-`.

A ref longer than 16 characters, or a payee holding `/` or `?`, raises `FormatError`. With no
transactions, `dump` returns `""`. The format loses the category; a ref of `NONREF` reads back as
None. A memo that runs over lines reads back joined with single spaces.
